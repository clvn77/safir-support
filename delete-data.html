Request deletion of your Safir data

Safir: Prayer & Qibla is provided by independent developer Denis Butakov. You do not need a Safir account to make a deletion request.

Send a request

  1. Email safir.app.support@gmail.com with the subject Safir data deletion request.
  2. Tell us whether you want your support correspondence, subscription service records, or both deleted. For support messages, write from the same email address you used previously. For subscription records, include the store used and an order or transaction reference if available so we can locate the relevant record.
  3. We may ask for additional information to verify that the record belongs to you. We will confirm the outcome by email and explain any records that must be retained and their retention period.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or full payment-card details. If your email app does not open, send the request using Gmail in your browser, or copy the address into your preferred email service.

Data deleted and data retained

After we verify and locate your records, we delete the requested support messages and attachments and can delete the associated customer record held by our subscription service provider, RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Information needed for an ongoing subscription may be retained until that subscription ends. Records required by law may be retained for the legally required period. If either exception applies, we will explain the reason and applicable period in our response. We retain correspondence needed to resolve the deletion request while it is being processed. There is no automatic deletion of all user data within 90 days.

Remove information stored on your device

Your display name, favorite duas, prayer settings and notification preferences are stored locally. On Android, open your device settings, select Safir, then Storage and Clear storage (wording varies by device). On iPhone, delete Safir using Delete App. Device backups may retain separate copies; manage these through your device backup settings.

Store and Google service records

Deleting app data or a RevenueCat record does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately through Google Play or Apple subscription settings.

Apple, Google Play and Google Maps/Places manage their own payment or service records under their policies. Safir cannot directly delete those providers' independently controlled records. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy for their privacy choices.

For more details, read the Safir Privacy Policy.
