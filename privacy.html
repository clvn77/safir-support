Safir Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Safir: Prayer & Qibla is provided by independent developer Denis Butakov. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Safir, including its subscription features.

Contact: safir.app.support@gmail.com

Information on your device

Safir does not require a Safir account. Your optional display name, prayer calculation method, prayer notification preferences, onboarding status and favorite duas are stored locally on your device. Safir does not send your display name or favorite duas to RevenueCat.

Prayer times and the Qibla bearing are calculated on your device. Compass sensor readings are used to display the direction. Prayer reminders are scheduled as local notifications rather than messages sent from a Safir server.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your backup settings.

Location and nearby places

With your permission, Safir accesses your device location while you use the app to calculate prayer times, show the Qibla direction and help you find nearby places. Safir does not request continuous background location access or maintain a location-history service.

Location coordinates may be processed by your device's location and geocoding services to identify your city. Explore uses Google Maps and Google Places. Map information, nearby-search coordinates (your location or a selected search area), search categories and place identifiers are processed by Google to display maps and retrieve place details. Google may also process technical information such as your IP address and device or service information under its own privacy policy.

You can change location permission in your device settings. Denying access limits features that depend on your current location. Explore also allows searching a selected map area.

Subscriptions

Apple handles App Store payments. Safir uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, determine subscription access, restore purchases and understand subscription activity. RevenueCat processes an automatically generated app user identifier, purchase and subscription records, and information needed to communicate with its service. Safir does not supply your name or email address to RevenueCat and does not receive your full payment-card details.

Subscription information can remain after uninstalling Safir so that purchases can be validated and restored. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel it through your store account.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle privacy requests. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary sensitive details.

Advertising and disclosure

Safir does not display advertisements or use an advertising analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Service providers described above process information to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and your choices

Local preferences remain until you change them or remove the app's local data. Device backups may retain copies. Support correspondence and subscription records are retained as needed to provide support, maintain subscription access and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Provider retention is also governed by the policies linked below.

You can disable location or notification permissions in your device settings, edit your display name and favorites in Safir, and remove local app data. Where applicable law provides rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to restrict or object to processing, contact us using the email above. We may need information that helps locate the relevant support or subscription record. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or ongoing subscriptions.

Service providers and external links

Providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their practices and safeguards:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

When you open a place website, directions or another external link, the destination service handles information under its own policy.

Children

Safir is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through support so that we can address it.

Updates

We may update this policy when Safir's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
