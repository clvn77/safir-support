SAFIR: PRAYER & QIBLA

How can we help?

For questions about Safir, prayer settings, subscriptions or restoring a purchase, email our support address.

safir.app.support@gmail.com

If your email app does not open, write using Gmail in your browser, or copy the address above into your preferred email service.

When contacting support

Please include your device model, app version and a description of what happened. Screenshots can help. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

Manage your subscription

For App Store purchases, open Apple subscription settings to manage or cancel your plan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

To recover access on another installation, use Restore Purchases in Safir with the same store account.

Privacy and terms

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details about your data, subscription access and services used by Safir.
